1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the "67 Clicker" mobile application (the "App") handles information when you use our service. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Local Data Only

67 Clicker is designed with privacy in mind. The App collects and stores the following data only on your device:

  • Total Click Count: Your cumulative clicking progress
  • Monthly Challenge Progress: Your progress toward the monthly 18,000 click goal
  • Badge Achievements: Records of badges earned (Bronze, Silver, Gold)
  • VS Mode Session Data: Click counts during local multiplayer sessions
  • App Settings: Timer duration preferences and other app configurations
  • Purchase History: Records of in-app purchases (stored locally)

2.2 Game Center Data

When you sign in to Apple's Game Center:

  • Your click scores are submitted to Game Center leaderboards
  • Scores are synced across your devices when using the same Game Center account
  • Your Game Center display name may be visible on leaderboards
  • This data is handled by Apple according to their Privacy Policy

2.3 In-App Purchase Data

When you make in-app purchases:

  • Purchase transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store
  • We do not collect or store payment information
  • Purchase records are stored locally on your device
  • Apple's privacy policy applies to payment processing

2.4 No Personal Information Collected

We do not collect:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Contact details
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers (except through Game Center, handled by Apple)
  • Usage analytics or tracking
  • Any data transmitted to our external servers (we have no external servers)

3. How We Use Information

The data collected is used solely to:

  • Maintain your clicking progress between app sessions
  • Track monthly challenge progress
  • Display badge achievements
  • Provide VS mode functionality
  • Sync scores across devices via Game Center
  • Process in-app purchases
  • Enhance your gaming experience

4. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using iOS's built-in storage systems (UserDefaults). No data is transmitted to external servers or third parties, except:

  • Game Center scores (handled by Apple)
  • In-app purchase transactions (handled by Apple)

5. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties, except:

  • Apple Game Center: Your scores and Game Center display name are shared through Apple's Game Center service for leaderboard functionality
  • Apple App Store: Purchase transactions are processed by Apple

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to any third parties.

6. Data Security

Since all data remains on your device (except Game Center data), it is protected by:

  • Your device's built-in security measures
  • iOS encryption and security features
  • Apple's Game Center security protocols

We recommend keeping your device updated and using appropriate security measures, including device passcodes or biometric authentication.

7. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Apple Game Center: For leaderboard and score syncing functionality. Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store: For in-app purchase processing. Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy

We do not integrate with any analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

8. Children's Privacy

Our App is suitable for all ages and does not collect any personal information from users of any age. All data is stored locally on the device. Game Center functionality is subject to Apple's age restrictions and parental controls.

9. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your information. You can:

  • Delete the App to remove all associated local data
  • Reset your progress through device settings or by deleting and reinstalling the App
  • Control Game Center data sharing through iOS Settings → Game Center
  • Disable in-app purchases through iOS Screen Time settings
  • Request deletion of Game Center data through Apple's support channels

10. Data Retention

Data is retained on your device until:

  • You delete the App
  • You reset your device
  • You manually clear app data through iOS settings

We do not retain any data on our servers as we do not operate any external servers.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Posting the updated policy on our legal information page

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us through:

  • The App Store developer contact information
  • Our legal information page at https://hot-app-uv-temp.github.io/67-clicker-legal/

13. Compliance

This privacy policy complies with applicable privacy laws and regulations, including:

  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation): By minimizing data collection and ensuring user control
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act): By not selling data and providing transparency
  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act): By not collecting personal information from children

We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of privacy protection.
