Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the "67 Clicker" app handles information when you use our service. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting your personal information.

Information We Collect

Local Data (Stored on Your Device)

67 Clicker stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Total Click Count: Your cumulative clicking progress in single-player mode
  • VS Mode Session Data: Temporary click counts during local multiplayer sessions (not stored permanently)
  • Game Settings: VS mode timer duration preferences

Apple Game Center Data

Optional Feature: If you choose to use the leaderboard feature, the app integrates with Apple Game Center.

When you sign into Game Center, the following occurs:

  • Authentication: Handled entirely by Apple through your Apple ID
  • Leaderboard Score: Your total click count is submitted to Apple's Game Center servers
  • Display Name: Your Game Center display name is visible on the leaderboard
  • Privacy Controls: Managed through your iOS Settings → Game Center

We do not collect or store any Game Center data. All Game Center data is managed by Apple in accordance with their privacy policy.

Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Contact details or address book information
  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, device ID)
  • Usage analytics or behavioral tracking
  • Photos, camera, or microphone access
  • Payment or financial information
  • Any data to our own servers (we don't have any)

How We Use Information

The data collected is used solely to:

  • Maintain your clicking progress between app sessions (stored locally)
  • Enable VS Mode multiplayer gameplay (local device only, not stored)
  • Submit your high score to Game Center leaderboard (if you opt in)
  • Enhance your gaming experience

Data Storage and Transmission

Local Storage

Your click count and preferences are stored locally on your device using iOS's built-in storage system (UserDefaults). This data never leaves your device except when submitted to Game Center (see below).

Game Center

If you sign into Game Center:

  • Your total click count is transmitted to Apple's Game Center servers
  • This data is used only for leaderboard rankings
  • Apple's privacy policy governs how this data is handled
  • You can control Game Center privacy in iOS Settings

No Third-Party Servers

We do not operate any servers or databases. All data remains on your device or (optionally) with Apple Game Center.

Data Security

Since all data remains on your device, it is protected by:

  • Your device's built-in security measures (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID)
  • iOS's app sandboxing and security architecture
  • Apple's encryption for data at rest

For Game Center data, Apple implements industry-standard security measures. We recommend:

  • Keeping your iOS device updated to the latest version
  • Using strong device security (passcode/biometric authentication)
  • Reviewing your Game Center privacy settings in iOS Settings

Third-Party Services

Apple Game Center

The only third-party service integrated into the app is Apple Game Center, which is optional. Apple Game Center is governed by Apple's privacy policy:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Game Center Privacy Information

No Advertising or Analytics

We do not use:

  • Advertising networks
  • Analytics services
  • Crash reporting services
  • Social media integrations
  • Any other third-party data collection tools

Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for users of all ages (4+) and does not collect any personal information from users of any age. Game Center use is optional and controlled by parental restrictions in iOS.

Parents can:

  • Disable Game Center in iOS Screen Time settings
  • Control who can see their child's Game Center profile
  • Monitor their child's app usage through Screen Time

Your Rights and Choices

You have complete control over your information:

Local Data

  • Delete All Data: Delete the app to remove all locally stored data
  • Reset Progress: Uninstall and reinstall the app to start fresh
  • No Account Required: Use the app without Game Center sign-in

Game Center Data

  • Opt Out: Don't sign into Game Center or sign out in iOS Settings
  • Control Visibility: Manage who can see your scores in Game Center settings
  • Delete Game Center Data: Contact Apple Support to remove leaderboard data

Data Retention

  • Local Data: Retained until you delete the app or reset your device
  • Game Center Data: Retained by Apple according to their policies
  • VS Mode Data: Temporary session data is cleared when you exit VS mode

International Users

The app is available worldwide. Your local data stays on your device regardless of location. Game Center data is handled by Apple according to your region's applicable laws.
