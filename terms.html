6. Monthly Challenge

The App features a Monthly Challenge where users must reach 18,000 clicks within a calendar month to unlock rewards:

  • Premium black background for the remainder of the month

  • Progress is tracked locally on your device

7. Badges and Awards

The App features a badge system:

  • Bronze Badge: Awarded at 25,000 total clicks
  • Silver Badge: Awarded at 50,000 total clicks
  • Gold Badge: Awarded at 100,000 total clicks

Badges are displayed on the leaderboard and in the app interface. Badge status is determined by your total click count.
